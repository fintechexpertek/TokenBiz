# TokenBiz
A platform where users may buy and sell tokens issued by startups.  Token issuers will get funds for business development, and buyers may get profit. The Platform will get its commission. Issuer – existing companies and founders who would like to register a company. We will also assist with company formation, if necessary.
Issuers – existing companies and founders who would like to register a company. 
Buyers – individuals and legal entities with full rights and legal capacity.

Steps of the Project creation: 
1) Company formation in BVI and license obtaining.
2) Platform creation and testing (in parallel with cl. 1).
3) Marketing campaign.
4) Operational activity.
5) Operational analysis.

Payment methods - Payments in SOL.
